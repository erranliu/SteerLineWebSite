AI can explore code, propose changes, and help reason about a problem. It can also misunderstand requirements, overlook context, invent details, or produce plausible but incorrect solutions. Performance varies by model, task, and available context.

Give the agent relevant context and a bounded objective. Ask for evidence, run appropriate checks, inspect the changes, and verify important claims independently. Use human judgment for ambiguity and decisions with consequences.
